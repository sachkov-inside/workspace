# Conventions

- Facts, hypotheses and owner decisions remain visibly separate; volatile provider claims have source and retrieval date.
- Update an existing authority instead of adding a parallel document.
- Membership product/ops authority lives here; content production authority lives in `sachkov-content`.
- Content portfolio records pillars, priorities, status and authority links, not scripts or publication artifacts.
- `Inside` is a working naming direction, not approved public branding.
- Tribute is only a candidate until a source-backed launch decision.
- Telegram-first launch must not silently depend on the future application.
- Task content changes use a task worktree; owner merges `main` unless exact AFK permission applies.
